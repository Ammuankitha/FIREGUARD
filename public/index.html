Evidence gaps & research priorities

These are limitations identified from the scope of the 12 investigation groups currently curated in this prototype and the NASA background material reviewed. They are research priorities—not proof that NASA has never studied these topics.

01 · Partial-gravity transferThe curated set is dominated by microgravity investigations. Results cannot be treated as direct validation at lunar (about 0.16 g) or Martian (about 0.38 g) gravity. More condition-matched data and modelling are needed.
02 · Mission atmosphere matchingEvidence must be compared against the actual habitat oxygen fraction, total pressure, ventilation velocity, material and geometry. A finding from one test envelope may not transfer to another.
03 · Integrated detection & suppressionIndividual combustion studies do not by themselves validate a complete habitat fire-response system. Integrated tests of sensing, airflow, suppressant delivery and obstructed geometries remain a key engineering need.

How to read this section: the gaps are derived by comparing the current dashboard coverage with the questions a mission designer must answer. NASA lists additional programme investigations, including FM2 and FLARE; their current status and released datasets must be checked before drawing conclusions. Electric-field flame studies investigate electric-field effects, not direct partial-gravity simulation.

Why this matters — heritage incidents

Apollo 1 — 1967Crew died in a launch-pad fire inside a pure-oxygen capsule. Drove the move to low-oxygen pre-launch atmospheres and burn-resistant materials.
Apollo 13 — 1970Oxygen tank explosion crippled the service module en route to the Moon. The crew survived in the lunar module.
Mir — 1997Oxygen generator failure started a fire aboard the station during joint ISS preparations.

Earth spin-offs

Firefighter air tanks1970s adaptation of spacesuit life-support technology to improve firefighter breathing gear.
Dust-discriminating smoke detector1970s shuttle detector that separates smoke from dust, cutting nuisance alarms. Adopted in mining.
Firefighter attire1980s transfer of spacesuit materials into protective clothing for firefighters.
Fine water-mist extinguishersDeveloped for ISS; now a low-water-damage method for server rooms and archives.

How this was built

The dashboard is a prototype research-navigation and decision-support tool. The NASA Researcher’s Guide (NP-2015-10-034-JSC, October 2015) provides historical background; it is not a substitute for current investigation-level PSI records. Investigation summaries and ratings are preliminary team-curated content and must be checked against primary records. Scores are editorial relevance scores, not NASA measurements, hazard probabilities or validated safety predictions. Links open PSI search results for the investigation name so users can verify the source record.
